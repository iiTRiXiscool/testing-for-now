Terms & Conditions

Last updated:

These terms cover booking an appointment with Koxyos through this website. By sending a booking request, you agree to the points below.

How booking works

Selecting a barber, day, and time on this site prepares a message and opens WhatsApp for you to send it. Sending that message is a request, not a confirmed booking — your appointment is confirmed once the shop replies to you on WhatsApp saying so.

Availability

Times shown reflect the barber's usual working hours as set by the shop, but two people could request the same slot before the shop confirms either one. If your requested time is no longer available, the shop will offer you the nearest alternative.

Cancellations & lateness

  • If you need to cancel or reschedule, message the shop on WhatsApp as early as you can.
  • Arriving significantly late may mean your slot is given to someone else or your service is shortened.
  • Repeated no-shows may affect whether future booking requests are accepted.

Pricing

Prices listed on the site are current at the time of publishing but may change. The price confirmed by the shop at the time of your visit is the one that applies.

Age

Anyone under 18 should book with a parent or guardian's knowledge.

Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated from time to time; the version shown on this page is always the current one.
