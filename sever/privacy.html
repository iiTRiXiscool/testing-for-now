Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This page explains what happens to your information when you use this website to book an appointment with Koxyos.

What we collect

When you book an appointment, you're asked for your full name. That's it — we don't collect your email, payment details, or any other personal information through this site.

How it's used

Your name is used only to pre-fill a WhatsApp message to the shop, confirming which barber, day, and time you'd like. That message is sent from your own device, through WhatsApp, directly to the shop's number — this website itself does not store, log, or transmit your name anywhere else.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use cookies and does not run any analytics or advertising trackers at this time. If that changes in the future (for example, if we add visit analytics), this policy will be updated and a cookie notice will be added where required.

Schedule & shop data

The list of barbers, their working hours, and the shop's contact details are managed by the shop owner through a staff-only area of the booking page. This information isn't connected to your personal data in any way.

Third parties

The only third party involved is WhatsApp (Meta), which handles the message once you choose to send it. Their own privacy policy applies to that conversation.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to the shop directly by phone or WhatsApp — see the contact details on our homepage.
